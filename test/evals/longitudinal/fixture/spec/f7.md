Feature request 7: prices and invoices.

- `room price ROOM --hourly CENTS` sets the room's hourly price, an integer of at least 0. The room shows `hourly`; a room whose price was never set has `"hourly": 0`.
- Every booking shows `price`: its cost in cents, hourly price × minutes ÷ 60, rounded down. It uses the room's price at the moment the booking was confirmed; a later price change does not change it. A waiting request shows `"price": null` until it is confirmed.
- The current time is the value of the environment variable `ROOMBOOK_NOW` (format `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM`) when it is set, else the system clock. A cancellation records the current time.
- `invoice --who NAME --month YYYY-MM` prints `{"who", "month", "lines", "total"}`. `lines` has one object `{"id", "room", "start", "charge"}` per booking of NAME that starts in that month: a confirmed booking at its full price; a booking cancelled less than 24 hours before its start at half its price, rounded down. Other cancelled bookings and waiting requests are not listed. Lines are sorted by start, then by id. `total` is the sum of the charges. A person with no matching booking gets an empty `lines` and a `total` of 0, with exit 0: `--who` is not checked against existing names.
